Approach

How I work.

Empathy first.

My best work comes from environments of trust — where mistakes help us learn and teams feel safe. I prioritize authenticity and genuine care for the people I'm designing for and working alongside.

Systems thinking.

Every design decision reaches beyond the immediate interface. I focus on scalable patterns, data models, and journeys that hold up across products, teams, and regulatory contexts.

Lead with influence.

Large, global teams don't move through authority — they move through clear stories, grounded in facts, that align business objectives with user needs. I bring both, up the chain and across.

Process

From Ambiguity to Clarity

01

Discover

Immerse in the problem space through stakeholder interviews, user research, and data analysis. Identify the real question beneath the surface request.

02

Define

Synthesize findings into clear problem statements and design principles. Align the team around shared goals before any pixels are pushed.

03

Design

Explore widely, then converge. Move from rough concepts to high-fidelity flows, building within — and sometimes pushing — the design system.

04

Test & Deliver

Validate with real users, iterate fast, and partner closely with engineering to ship. Measure outcomes against the original goals.
